Problem Statement

Why a New Framework?

  • GRIHA and IGBC ratings focus primarily on buildings, not linear infrastructure
  • International rating systems don't align with India's regulatory and environmental conditions
  • No balanced, multi-dimensional sustainability rating exists for Indian infrastructure projects
  • Long corridor projects (highways, metro) have unique challenges unaddressed by existing tools

Methodology

Research Methodology

A six-step rigorous process from legislative review to validated weight calculation.

Step 1
01
🌿

Law Review

Reviewed RFCTLARR Act, Factories Act, URDPFI Guidelines, IRC Standards, IS 1893 to identify legally mandated sustainability criteria.

Step 2
02
🗂️

Framework Design

Structured a 4-level hierarchy — goal, dimensions, categories, sub-criteria — using expert panel discussions and literature mapping.

Step 3
03
🌐

Web Tool Development

Built this online AHP survey platform for remote expert participation, auto-matrix computation and live CR feedback.

Step 4
04
👩‍💼

Expert Survey

Collected responses from 12 infrastructure industry experts — planners, engineers, environmental consultants.

Step 5
05
📐

Weight Calculation

Aggregated responses using geometric mean and derived priority eigenvectors for each level of the hierarchy.

Step 6
06
✅

Consistency Check

Verified Consistency Ratio CR = 0.0137 — well below the 0.10 threshold — confirming judgment reliability.
